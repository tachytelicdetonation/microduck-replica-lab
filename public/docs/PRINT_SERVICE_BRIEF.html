Microduck: request for a fit-test and print quotation

I am making one personal Microduck replica and do not own a printer. Please quote a small fit-test batch first, then a full set after I approve the measured fit. This is a request template; no quote has been submitted by this project.

Files: extract microduck-fit-check.zip or microduck-print-quote.zip. Read the included print-order.csv. A file is included once; the quantity column controls the number of copies. The full set represents 30 design types and 36 pieces. Preserve left and right parts. All STL coordinates are millimetres, at 100% scale.

Please quote these process choices separately:

  • Rigid parts: PA12 nylon with MJF or SLS; an FDM PETG fit prototype is an alternative. Inspect the 1 mm reinforcement plates, narrow neck links, bearing seats and screw holes before accepting the job.
  • Soft parts: sole_left, sole_right, jaw_soft and soft_mouth_top, proposed TPU around 95A. State the hardness, process and any limitations. This material choice is a starting point for testing.
  • Review the m12_lens_holder against the purchased camera/lens; manufacturing route and thread compatibility are not established. Hold this item until clarified.

Please include:

  • Print price, setup/minimum fees, post-processing, shipping to my US address, taxes/duties and lead time.
  • Actual material/process, proposed orientation, layer height/infill if applicable, and dimensional tolerance. Flag unsupported walls, closed holes, trapped powder or support-removal problems.
  • Support removal/cleaning suitable for assembly, with no scaling, automatic hole filling, geometry changes or material substitutions without my review.
  • A way to identify the individual pieces, and measured dimensions and weights of the fit-test pieces.
  • Separate cost for any CAD/fit work, drilling/reaming, inserts, tapping or assembly. These operations are not authorized by a basic print quote.

The STLs come from a simulation model. They are not released manufacturing drawings and have no validated fit tolerances, material specification or insert pockets. I will test one actual XL330 servo, the selected bearing and screw stack with the first batch before ordering the remaining pieces. A completed print order does not establish a working robot.
